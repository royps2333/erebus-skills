# Community conduct

We want this to be a welcoming place to learn, question, and improve useful tools.
Everyone participating in this project's spaces is expected to follow these rules.

## How we work together

- Treat people with respect, including beginners and people who disagree with you.
- Critique ideas and code directly and constructively. Support technical claims
  with evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, and make room for corrections.
- Keep personal information, credentials, and confidential material private.
- Respect contributors' time. Participation is voluntary; no one owes an immediate
  reply, a fix, or acceptance of a proposal.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, sharing someone's
private information without consent, spam, and repeated disruptive demands are
not welcome. Good-faith disagreement and reports of defects are welcome.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may ask for a change in behavior, remove content, close or lock a
conversation, or block participation when needed. Serious abuse may lead to
immediate action. These expectations apply to maintainers too.

Use GitHub's reporting controls for abusive content where available. Do not repeat
private or harmful material in a public issue to report it. This project does not
currently offer a dedicated confidential conduct-reporting inbox. Security
vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).

Moderation and support are handled as maintainer availability permits; there is
no guaranteed response time.
